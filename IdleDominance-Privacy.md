# Privacy Policy

**Idle Dominance** (Idle Dominance: Map Conquest)  
Twelve52 LLC  
App identifier: com.twelve52.idledom

Last updated: October 4, 2026

Idle Dominance ("the App") is developed by Twelve52 LLC ("we", "us", "our"). This policy explains what information is collected when you play, by whom, and why. It covers the iPhone, iPad, Android and Steam versions.

## The short version

- No account and no personal details. The App never asks for your name, email address or anything else about you.
- No game server. Idle Dominance is a single-player game that plays fully offline.
- On iPhone, iPad and Android the App shows ads through Google AdMob and sends anonymous gameplay events to Google Firebase Analytics.
- On Steam the App collects nothing. The Steam version contains no ad software and no analytics software.
- We do not sell information about you.

## Game data

Your saves, settings and progress are stored on your device. If you are signed in to Game Center (iPhone and iPad), Google Play Games (Android) or Steam, your save is also stored in your own account with that service so you can continue on another device. We do not have access to it. Export Save and Import Save in Settings produce and read a save code that stays in your hands.

## Advertising (Google AdMob), iPhone, iPad and Android only

The App shows banner, interstitial and rewarded ads through Google AdMob. Google may collect device identifiers (including the advertising identifier, if you allow it), IP address and usage data to serve and measure ads, personalized or not depending on your choices. This data may be used to track you across apps and websites owned by other companies, and only with your consent.

- Where the law requires it (in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, for example) the App first shows Google's consent form. Without consent the App requests non-personalized ads only.
- On iPhone and iPad the App then asks for tracking permission through App Tracking Transparency before the ad software starts. Declining changes nothing about the game.
- Where the consent form applies, an Ad privacy options button appears in the App's Settings.
- Premium removes banner and interstitial ads for good. Rewarded ads are always optional: one only plays when you press its button.

Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

## Analytics (Google Firebase), iPhone, iPad and Android only

The App sends a short, fixed list of gameplay events to Google Firebase Analytics: tutorial steps, region and era milestones, Successions, Cycles, ad and store screen events, and a few setting changes. Firebase also records installs, sessions, device type and language. These are tied to an app instance identifier that Firebase generates, not to you.

- Events carry numbers and fixed ids only. No names, no email addresses, no text you type and no precise location.
- We use them to balance the game and to measure ad revenue. We build no profiles.
- Without consent, analytics collection is set to denied. Settings also has a switch that turns analytics off.

Firebase's privacy information: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

## Feedback and bug reports (optional), iPhone, iPad and Android only

If you choose to send feedback or report a bug from Settings, the text you type is sent to our report inbox (hosted by Supabase) together with the App build number, your platform, your device model, the App language and a short code describing your current game so we can reproduce a problem. No account, advertising identifier or other identifier is attached. Nothing identifies you unless you write it in the message.

## Game Center and Google Play Games

If you sign in, Apple or Google handles your profile, achievements, leaderboard entries and cloud save under its own privacy policy. We receive no personal details from either service.

## Purchases

Purchases are processed entirely by Apple, Google or Valve. We never see or store payment information.

## Notifications

Notifications are optional and stay off until you turn them on. They are scheduled on your device, at most two per day. There is no notification server and no notification token is sent to us.

## Time checks

After a device restart the App reads the current time from the internet so time away is credited fairly. The request sends no data about you.

## Steam

The Steam version shows no ads, has no in-app purchases, and sends no analytics, feedback or other data to us or to any advertising or analytics company. Steam Cloud, achievements and leaderboards are handled by Valve under its own privacy policy.

## Children

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. The App reads the age signal that the App Store or Google Play provides where one is available. A child signal turns ads and analytics off. A teen signal limits ads to non-personalized.

## Retention and deletion

Feedback and bug reports are deleted once resolved, or after 12 months at most. To have a report you sent deleted sooner, email us and tell us roughly when you sent it and what it said. Everything else lives on your device or in your own Game Center, Google Play Games or Steam account, and is removed when you delete the App or that account's data.

## Your choices

- Decline tracking on iPhone and iPad (Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking), or reset or delete your advertising ID on Android (Settings > Privacy > Ads).
- Open Ad privacy options in the App's Settings where they are offered.
- Turn analytics off in the App's Settings.
- Buy Premium to remove banner and interstitial ads.
- Leave notifications off.
- Play without signing in to Game Center or Google Play Games to keep your save on the device only.
