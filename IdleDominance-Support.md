Twelve52 LLC

Having trouble with Idle Dominance? Here are the quick answers, and how to reach us.

## Common questions

### How do I restore my purchase?

Open the App, go to Settings, and tap Restore Purchases. Premium comes back on the same Apple ID or Google account that bought it, and on family members' devices when Family Sharing is on. Purchases belong to the store account that made them and do not move between stores.

### What does Premium do?

Premium is a one-time purchase. It removes the banner and interstitial ads and doubles your empire's rate for good. Rewarded ads stay optional: one only plays when you press its button.

### My progress was lost. Can I get it back?

Saves live on your device. If you are signed in to Game Center, Google Play Games or Steam, your save is also kept in that account and is offered back after a reinstall or on a new device. Without a sign-in, a deleted App cannot be recovered.

### How do I move my save to another device or store?

Settings > Export Save gives you a save code or file. Settings > Import Save on the other device reads it. This works between iPhone, iPad, Android and Steam.

### Does the game need the internet?

No. Idle Dominance plays fully offline. A connection is only used for ads, cloud saves, leaderboards and a time check after your device restarts.

### The game is crashing or not loading.

Close the App completely and reopen it. Make sure you have the latest version from the store, then restart your device. If it keeps happening, send a bug report from Settings (below) or email us.

### How do I turn off notifications?

Notifications are off until you turn them on in the App's Settings, and you can turn them off there again. You can also turn notifications off for the App in your device settings.

### How do I turn off analytics or change my ad choices?

The App's Settings has a switch that turns analytics off, and an Ad privacy options button where a consent form applies. Tracking permission is in your device settings.

### Which devices are supported?

- iPhone and iPad with iOS 16 or later
- Phones and tablets with Android 8.0 or later
- Steam on Windows 10 or later and macOS 12 or later

## Send feedback or report a bug

On iPhone, iPad and Android: Settings > Send feedback / Report a bug. You type it, we read it. No account needed. On Steam, email us.
