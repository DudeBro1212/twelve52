# Privacy Policy

**Medieval Dominance**  
Twelve52 LLC  
App identifiers: com.twelve52.md (iPhone and iPad), com.twelve52.meddom (Android)

Last updated: October 23, 2026

Medieval Dominance ("the App") is developed by Twelve52 LLC ("we", "us", "our"). This policy explains what information is collected when you play, by whom, and why. It covers the iPhone, iPad and Android versions.

## The short version

- No account and no personal details. The App never asks for your name, email address or anything else about you.
- Your reign is stored on your device. The game plays fully offline.
- Rival Houses is optional. If you raise your banner, a snapshot of your in-game house is shared with other players under a random key made on your device.
- The App shows ads through Google AdMob. On iPhone and iPad, and only with your consent, it also sends gameplay measurements to Google Firebase Analytics.
- We do not sell information about you.

## Game data

Your saves, settings and progress are stored on your device. The home screen widget (iPhone and iPad) reads the same data on your device. If you delete the App, local save data is removed and cannot be recovered.

## Rival Houses (optional)

If you choose to raise your banner in Rival Houses, the App sends a snapshot of your in-game house to our game server (hosted by Supabase) so other players can see it on the lists and raid it: the house's name, coat of arms, ruler's in-game name, standing and army figures, together with a random identifier the App generates on your device. Raids you make, and raids made on your house, are recorded the same way.

- None of this includes your name, contact details, Apple, Game Center or Google identity, location or any device or advertising identifier, and it is not used for advertising or tracking.
- Lowering your banner stops new snapshots. Snapshots that are not updated for 60 days are removed, together with their raids.
- To have your house removed sooner, email us at the address below and include your house's name.

The App also reads game news and balance updates from the same server. Those requests send no information about you.

## Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App shows banner, interstitial and rewarded ads through Google AdMob. Google may collect device identifiers (including the advertising identifier, if you allow it), IP address and usage data to serve and measure ads, personalized or not depending on your choices. This data may be used to track you across apps and websites owned by other companies, and only with your consent.

- Where the law requires it (in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, and in US states with privacy laws, for example) the App first shows Google's consent form. Where this applies, you can change your choice at any time with Ad privacy options in the App's Settings.
- On iPhone and iPad the App asks for tracking permission through App Tracking Transparency. Declining changes nothing about the game.
- Remove Ads removes banner and interstitial ads for good. Rewarded ads are always optional: one only plays when you press its button.

Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

## Analytics (Google Firebase), iPhone and iPad only

After you consent through the form above, the App sends standard measurements to Google Firebase Analytics: installs, sessions, device type, language and ad revenue events. These are tied to an app instance identifier that Firebase generates, not to you. Nothing is collected before consent, and the Android version contains no analytics software.

Firebase's privacy information: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

## Game Center and Google Play Games

If you sign in, Apple or Google handles your profile, achievements and leaderboard entries under its own privacy policy. We receive no personal details from either service.

## Purchases

Purchases (Remove Ads, the Season Pass, season XP packs and era scenario packs) are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store or by Google through Google Play. We never see or store payment information.

## Notifications

Reminders are optional, scheduled on your device and sent by your device. There is no notification server and no notification token is sent to us.

## Children

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

## Your choices

- Play without raising your banner to keep your house off the lists.
- Where offered, Ad privacy options in the App's Settings.
- Decline tracking on iPhone and iPad (Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking), or reset or delete your advertising ID on Android (Settings > Privacy > Ads).
- Buy Remove Ads to remove banner and interstitial ads.
- Play without signing in to Game Center or Google Play Games.
